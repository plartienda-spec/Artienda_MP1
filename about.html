About LARPHarness

The aim is not to remove the effort of learning but rather allocate that effort better, so more of it goes into the thinking that builds real understanding.

Idea & Philosophy

LearnHarness was created by Amos Blomqvist as an open-source agent harness for learning (now over 9.7k stars on GitHub). The best explanations and practice problems depend on both what you already understand and how you reason about it.

Our coursework showcase LARPHarness highlights that workflow: agents probe your current mental model, plan a minimal dependency tree, teach one step at a time (predict → teach → check), and extract propositions into one persistent graph shared across sessions.

Core Agent Loop

Probe & Plan

Finds where your understanding ends and builds a minimal lesson map from known concepts to your goal.

Predict, Teach, Check

You predict the next move before seeing the explanation, surfacing errors immediately while subagents fact-check or build demos.

Shared Context Graph

Propositions and reasoning chains stay saved locally so future teaching and practice sessions build on prior work.
